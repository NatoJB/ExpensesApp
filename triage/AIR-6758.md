## Likely root causes

Stated as hypotheses — I had no access to Air or Junie source (see below), so these are inferred
from issue history and should be confirmed before planning work.

**Hypothesis 1 — Junie isn't wired into an effort control Air already has.**
Prigara's phrase *"similarly to other agents"* implies other Air agents already expose a thinking
/ effort level, and Junie is the outlier. If so this is integration plumbing, not new product
design: surface the control for Junie in the model selector, then pass the selected level into
the Junie agent invocation. Cheapest thing to check first, and it would confirm the
`Agent Integration` subsystem.

**Hypothesis 2 — AIR-6757 and AIR-6758 are the same bug.**
Junie has historically surfaced reasoning depth as a *per-model variant* ("Think More") rather
than an orthogonal effort axis — that is exactly what AIR-4557's title describes. If Air
enumerates Junie models as a flat catalog and that catalog is incomplete (AIR-6757: *"AIR
currently exposes a much smaller set of models than Junie"*), then the "Think More" variants are
simply missing from the list, and **one catalog fix resolves both issues**. This is cheap to
falsify: diff the model list Air shows for Junie against the list Junie shows in the IDE. If the
`Think More` entries are absent there, close AIR-6758 as a duplicate of AIR-6757.

I'd test Hypothesis 2 first — it's the cheapest check and has the largest payoff.
